Power BI dashboards and business intelligence in Papua New Guinea

Leaders need timely insight, not days of manual spreadsheet work. We turn your data into dashboards and reports they can act on.

Talk to us about this service What we offer
The challenge

Sound familiar?

Many organisations in Papua New Guinea and the Pacific face the same practical problems:

  • Monthly reports that take days of copying and pasting between spreadsheets
  • Different teams reporting different numbers for the same measure
  • Plenty of data collected, but little of it used in management decisions
  • No clear set of indicators linking daily work to strategic goals
What we offer

Business intelligence & analytics services

Every engagement is scoped to your objectives, budget, systems and capacity. We choose tools that fit, not the most complex option.

Executive dashboards

Power BI dashboards and management reports for strategic, operational and program performance, designed around the decisions your leaders make.

Performance measurement systems

KPI frameworks, indicator dictionaries, scorecards, reporting calendars and performance review processes.

Data analysis and insights

Trend, segmentation, variance and cohort analysis that explains what is happening and why.

Reporting automation

Repeatable data preparation and scheduled refresh that cut manual reporting time.

Decision intelligence

We translate analysis into options, implications, risks and recommended actions for leaders and boards.

What you get

Typical deliverables

Deliverables are agreed in writing before work starts, and every engagement ends with a handover so your team can carry on.

  • Dashboard or report prototype, then the finished product
  • KPI framework and indicator dictionary
  • Documented data model and business rules
  • Testing record and user guide
  • Handover and user training
