Embedded data advisory and fractional data leadership in Papua New Guinea

You should not need a full data team before you know what you need. We provide ongoing data expertise without the overhead.

Talk to us about this service What we offer
The challenge

Sound familiar?

Many organisations in Papua New Guinea and the Pacific face the same practical problems:

  • Not enough data work to justify a full-time senior data role
  • Dashboards and reports that stall once the original builder leaves
  • A data strategy on paper that never turns into action
  • Staff unsure who to ask when data questions come up
What we offer

Embedded data advisory services

Every engagement is scoped to your objectives, budget, systems and capacity. We choose tools that fit, not the most complex option.

Fractional data leadership

Part-time strategic guidance on data priorities, governance, architecture, capability and executive reporting.

Embedded analytics support

A supplementary or outsourced data function covering analysis, dashboards, reporting and data quality monitoring.

Ongoing BI and reporting support

We maintain your reports, improve measures, support users and deliver scheduled insights.

Data strategy implementation

We turn strategy and assessment recommendations into workplans, governance routines and deliverable projects.

What you get

Typical deliverables

Deliverables are agreed in writing before work starts, and every engagement ends with a handover so your team can carry on.

  • Agreed monthly hours, deliverables and response times
  • Regular management insights and reports
  • Maintained dashboards and data quality checks
  • Governance advice as issues arise
  • Skills transfer to your staff
