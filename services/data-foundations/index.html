Service 01 · Data foundations & transformation

Data foundations and transformation in Papua New Guinea

Good decisions start with data you can find and trust. We assess where your data stands, identify gaps and build a solid data foundation.

Talk to us about this service What we offer
The challenge

Sound familiar?

Many organisations in Papua New Guinea and the Pacific face the same practical problems:

  • Records kept on paper or in many separate spreadsheets
  • Inconsistent formats, definitions and identifiers across teams and systems
  • Incomplete, duplicated or out-of-date records with no clear owner
  • Reports that depend on one or two people who know where everything is
What we offer

Data foundations & transformation services

Every engagement is scoped to your objectives, budget, systems and capacity. We choose tools that fit, not the most complex option.

Data maturity assessment

A review of your data strategy, governance, people, processes, systems, quality and analytics, with a maturity scorecard, gap analysis and prioritised roadmap.

Data audit and health check

An inventory of your datasets and reports, checking completeness, consistency, duplication, accessibility, ownership and risk.

Data cleaning and standardisation

We profile, validate, de-duplicate and standardise your data, identifiers, reference lists and business definitions.

Excel to Insight

We turn spreadsheet-based reporting into structured datasets, repeatable processes and decision-ready dashboards.

Data governance frameworks

Clear data ownership, stewardship, standards, quality rules, classification, access, retention and accountability.

Data architecture and digitisation

Practical current-state and target-state designs, digitisation pathways and low-cost data pipelines that fit your budget and connectivity.

What you get

Typical deliverables

Deliverables are agreed in writing before work starts, and every engagement ends with a handover so your team can carry on.

  • Maturity scorecard and gap analysis
  • Data inventory and data dictionary
  • Data quality assessment
  • Governance framework, standards and responsibility matrix
  • Current-state and target-state diagrams
  • Prioritised implementation roadmap and executive briefing
