Service 06 · Interoperability & data sharing

Data sharing and interoperability in Papua New Guinea

Agencies and partners can only work together when their numbers line up. We help them share data safely and consistently.

Talk to us about this service What we offer
The challenge

Sound familiar?

Many organisations in Papua New Guinea and the Pacific face the same practical problems:

  • Agencies and partners using different definitions for the same measure
  • Data shared by email with no agreed rules on use or access
  • Systems that cannot connect, so information is re-entered by hand
  • Costly integration projects started before the basics are agreed
What we offer

Interoperability & data sharing services

Every engagement is scoped to your objectives, budget, systems and capacity. We choose tools that fit, not the most complex option.

Data-sharing frameworks

Governance, roles, standards, permitted uses, access controls and responsible data exchange processes.

Interoperability roadmaps

An assessment of system and data fragmentation, with phased options for connecting information.

Common data definitions and standards

Agreement on identifiers, reference data, business rules, metadata and reporting definitions.

What you get

Typical deliverables

Deliverables are agreed in writing before work starts, and every engagement ends with a handover so your team can carry on.

  • Data-sharing framework and agreement templates
  • Common definitions and data standards
  • Current-state map of systems and data flows
  • Phased interoperability roadmap
  • Governance and responsibility matrix
