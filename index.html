About us

A Pacific advisory firm built on trust

DataSense Pasifik Advisory is a business advisory services firm. We work remotely, with the flexibility to serve clients across Papua New Guinea and the Pacific, bringing together data, analytics, governance and financial expertise.

We believe data and technology should serve people and communities. That means being honest about methods and limits, protecting the information entrusted to us, and contributing to sustainable development and social wellbeing in the region.

  • Government
  • Private sector
  • Development partners
  • NGOs
  • Research institutions
  • Communities

Our purpose

To use data, analytics and advisory services to contribute positively to sustainable development and social wellbeing in Papua New Guinea and the Pacific.

Our way of working: professional, transparent and accountable – grounded in respect for local customs, leadership structures and collective wellbeing.

How we work

A clear path from first conversation to results

Every engagement follows a structured process so expectations, responsibilities and data-handling arrangements are clear from day one.

  1. Initial conversation

    Tell us about your organisation, goals and timelines. We confirm we are the right fit.

  2. Discovery

    We meet with you and your stakeholders to understand needs, data, risks and community considerations.

  3. Proposal

    A clear scope of work, methodology, deliverables, timeline, team and pricing.

  4. Agreement & kickoff

    Contracts, confidentiality and data-sharing arrangements are agreed, then work begins with a joint kickoff.

  5. Delivery & review

    Regular progress reporting, open communication, and a final review of what worked.

Our commitments

Responsible with your information

Our team works under a Code of Conduct and Ethics, an Information Security Policy and a Data Governance Standard Operating Procedure. Here is what that means for you.

Confidentiality

Client information is shared only on a need-to-know basis, under signed confidentiality agreements that continue after our work ends.

Security

Secure storage, access controls, multi-factor authentication where feasible, and prompt incident reporting.

Data minimisation

We collect and use only the data a project needs, and securely return or dispose of it when it is no longer required.

Cultural respect

We respect local customs, community sensitivities and traditional knowledge, and promote informed participation.

Integrity

No bribes, kickbacks or improper influence. Conflicts of interest are declared and managed.

Transparency

We are clear about our methods, assumptions and limitations, and we communicate findings accurately.

Fairness & inclusion

We guard against bias and consider the impact of data and technology on vulnerable groups.

Continuous learning

We keep our skills current in data governance, cybersecurity, analytics and emerging technology.
